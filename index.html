The Palace

A part of the Maharaja's palace, 400 years on

Hotel Paradise is located in the part of the palace of the Maharaja of Jaisalmer which was formerly used for the accommodation of the royal family's guests. The building as it stands today is about 400 years old.

The rooms, which are cool in summer and sheltered in winter, are centred around a shady garden courtyard with bougainvillea trees. Rooms next to the wall have a view of the desert and Garisar Lake. Breakfast and drinks are served on the rooftop terrace.

The hotel sits just off the main square of Jaisalmer Fort, on entering through the 4th gate. Camel safaris, jeep excursions to villages, forts, temples and other places of interest in the desert can all be arranged, and bus and train tickets can be booked too.

The owner Captain Chandra, the head manager and the rest of the staff will be happy to give you any assistance you need and will do their utmost to make your stay an enjoyable and memorable holiday. Captain Chandra Shripat, owner
